What changed, and why

Three questions, three rows

Lens (what's on the board), Connect (into the next chord), Board (the instrument). Tuning and Labels move out of "Palette" — they were never a chord-scale decision — and the fixed label rail makes the panel scannable top to bottom.

Radios look like radios, modifiers like switches

The nine-way exclusive choice is a chip row where the one selected chip fills solid. Altered and Bebop chromatic become real toggle switches — today all eleven look like the same button, hiding which are either/or and which stack.

Sub-settings hang off their parent

The 3:2 level ladder, its Shape filter, and Voice Leading's Hunt row sit on an indent rail directly under the choice that owns them, appearing only when that choice is live — no orphaned rows, nothing lit that isn't doing anything.

State reads back as a sentence

The header chip says what the board is doing in words — and instead of an italic paragraph explaining that 3:2 sidelines Melody and Fret focus, those controls simply disable with a short hint. Try switching the lens to 3:2 System above.
